§ 02Use them in your designs3 steps

Use them anywhere

Every font here is free on Google Fonts. Here’s how to get one into your app.

  1. 1

    Download it

    Click a font name to open it on Google Fonts, then press Get font → Download all. You get a ZIP of font files.

  2. 2

    Install it

    Unzip it and open the .ttf file. On a computer, press Install. In PixelLab, use Text → Font → Import font on your phone.

  3. 3

    Use it

    Restart Photoshop, Illustrator or Affinity and the font appears in the list. Canva and Photopea already include most Google Fonts. Just search the name.

  4. 4

    Pair it well

    Use the heading font for titles only and the body font for longer text. Stick to two fonts per design.
